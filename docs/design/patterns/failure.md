FAIL-RETRY also requires R `deadlineOutcome:Observable`. Its filled fixture supplies Timeout. Deadline is measured from the initial attempt start.

FAIL-CANCEL also requires R `maximumWait:Duration`, `onFailure:Observable`. Its filled fixture supplies 10ms and CancellationUnconfirmed.

These additional required slots are part of their entry definitions. The negative cases preserve them unchanged.

| Additional retry case | Filled assignments | Expected result and incomplete counterpart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RETRY-NONEMPTY | operation=TryAt(attemptIndex), retryCount=1, eligible=result is Error(Temporary), delay=0ms, deadline=1000ms, duplicatePolicy=no external effects, exhaustion=last error, deadlineOutcome=Timeout | With each call completing at 1ms, attempts 0 and 1 produce Error(Temporary), then Ok(unit). No third call occurs. Remove duplicatePolicy: missing-required at duplicatePolicy. |

At exact deadline equality, expiry wins. Every timing comparison uses the declared monotonic elapsed clock.

For cleanup, a cleanup-only failure returns that error under every policy. Preserve-primary retains the primary error and records the cleanup error.

Replace-primary returns the cleanup error with the primary retained in history. Combine returns the ordered pair of primary and cleanup errors.
