EXT-PERSIST requires R `unknown:Observable`. Its fixture supplies OutcomeUnknown. Delete uses the same missing and unknown policies as read/write.

EXT-IDEMPOTENCY requires R `deadline:PositiveDuration` and `unknown:Observable`. Its fixture supplies 1000ms and OutcomeUnknown.

EXT-TRANSACTION requires R `unknown:Observable`. Its fixture supplies CommitUnknown. Abort failure retains that failure and known effects instead of claiming Aborted.

Endpoint, Store, ConsistencyPolicy, AtomicityPolicy, and IdempotencyPolicy reference exact contract definitions with admitted inputs, observables, failures, and limits.

These policies are not opaque proof assertions. Missing operational meaning creates a decision or unsupported-method finding.

The fixture services are illustrative finite state relations. No real payment, database, transport, or exactly-once network guarantee is claimed.

The compensation fixture's charge-result@f1 is a hypothetical retained ActionResult with known effect C. No future runtime ID is invented during authoring.
